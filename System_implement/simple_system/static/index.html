Final lengthening factors — how much each kind of phrase ending is stretched (×1.0 = not at all)

EndingExampleStretch ×
Used by live chat and by the third row of the Phrase chunking card (★×factor marks stretched chunks). Each stretched chunk gets its ending's factor plus a random variation, so the stretch differs from chunk to chunk and between conversations. The order (deeper boundaries and ね longer than よ) follows Den 2015 and a 2025 J. Japanese Linguistics study; the values are our own choices, tune them by ear. Every AI line in the saved log records its kind and factor. The A/B card below stretches every eligible ending with one factor, to hear the effect.
